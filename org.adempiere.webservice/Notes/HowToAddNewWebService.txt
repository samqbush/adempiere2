How to add a SOAP operation

The approved WSDL files under contracts/xfire-v1/wsdl are the public contract.
Do not generate or publish a replacement WSDL at runtime.

1. Add the operation and XMLBeans types to the applicable approved WSDL/XSD.
2. Regenerate generated_xmlbeans.jar and its sources with the module's xmlbean
   Ant target.
3. Add an explicit ServiceOperationKey and handler binding to
   SoapOperationRegistry and BusinessSoapDispatcher.
4. Implement transport-neutral behavior under src/business/java. Report SOAP
   faults with ServiceFault; business code must not import CXF or servlet APIs.
5. Add the CXF provider/codec handling needed to preserve the exact static WSDL
   wire shape.
6. Add baseline, authenticated/fault, and applicable state-delta fixtures under
   contracts/xfire-v1/operations, then regenerate manifest.sha256.
7. Run the frozen-contract, registry, modern-WAR, and installed public-path
   gates before publishing the operation.

Both historical public URL forms remain owned by SoapCompatibilityRouter. The
Tomcat 10.1 CXF connector stays loopback-only.
